Pillar Bank is a legacy built by Thomas and Clyde Rossman in 1849. One of the only major US banks founded in California, Pillar became the first private bank to offer wealth management services to people during the gold rush, managing the modern equivalent of $2.8 billion in private assets.

Today, we hold the same values as we did 170 years ago with even more expertise, experienced advisors, and modern technology to make wealth management simple and effective.
